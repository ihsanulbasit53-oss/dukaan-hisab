📚 Shopkeeper Guide

Dukaan chalane mein sirf sale karna kaafi nahi hota. Dukandaar ko purchase price, selling price, profit, expenses aur stock ka bhi sahi hisab rakhna chahiye.

1. Profit kaise calculate karein?

Profit = Selling Price − Buying Price

2. Discount kaise calculate karein?

Discount customer ko original price par percentage ki bunyaad par diya jata hai. Discount dene se pehle apna profit zaroor check karein.

3. Stock ka hisab kyun zaroori hai?

Stock ki total cost aur possible selling value maloom hone se dukandaar ko apne business ki actual position samajhne mein madad milti hai.

4. Udhaar ka record kyun rakhein?

Udhaar ka proper record cash flow ko manage karne aur customers se remaining payment recover karne mein madad karta hai.

5. Business expenses ka hisab

Rent, electricity, transport, staff salary aur doosre monthly expenses ko record karna zaroori hai taake net profit ka andaza ho sake.
