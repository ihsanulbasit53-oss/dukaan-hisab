About Dukaan Hisab

Simple Business Calculators for Pakistan

What is Dukaan Hisab?

Dukaan Hisab is a simple and free online calculator website designed to help shopkeepers and small business owners with everyday business calculations.

Our goal is to make common business calculations easier, faster and more understandable, especially when using a mobile phone.

What can you calculate?

Dukaan Hisab currently provides calculators for:

  • Profit calculation
  • Discount calculation
  • Profit margin
  • Stock value
  • Monthly sales and estimated profit
  • Business expenses and net profit
  • Udhaar / remaining balance
  • Wholesale and retail pricing

Our Purpose

Running a small shop requires keeping track of buying prices, selling prices, profit, expenses and stock. Dukaan Hisab aims to provide simple tools that can make these calculations easier.

We want to continue adding useful tools and practical information that can help shopkeepers manage their everyday calculations more easily.

Important Note

The calculators on Dukaan Hisab provide estimates for general business calculations. They are not a replacement for professional accounting, tax or financial advice.
